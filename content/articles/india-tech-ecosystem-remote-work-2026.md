## The Transformation of India's Engineering Landscape

Over the past three years, India's tech ecosystem has undergone a profound structural shift. What was once predominantly an offshore IT services hub has matured into a powerhouse of product engineering, Global Capability Centers (GCCs), and world-class AI research teams.

Simultaneously, the normalization of global asynchronous remote work allows Indian software engineers to build foundational infrastructure for Silicon Valley startups while living in Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Pune, Gurugram, or Tier-2 cities.

## The Rise of Global Capability Centers (GCCs)

More than 1,700 global multinationals now operate GCCs in India, not for mundane support, but for core IP development. From Goldman Sachs and JP Morgan in Bengaluru to Uber, Microsoft, and Google in Hyderabad, engineering leaders are managing mission-critical distributed systems locally.

Key drivers include:
- Unmatched scale of senior TypeScript, Rust, Python, and cloud engineers.
- Maturing product management leadership with 10+ years of hypergrowth startup experience.
- Cost-to-impact leverage: a company can build a 10-person elite AI infrastructure team in Bengaluru for the cost of two mid-level engineers in San Francisco.

## Remote Work Infrastructure: Taxes, Contracts & Invoicing

Working remotely for global companies requires understanding the regulatory framework:

1. **Contractor via Deel / Remote / Oyster:** Most global startups hire Indian engineers as independent contractors or through Employer of Record (EOR) entities.
2. **Presumptive Taxation (Section 44ADA):** For independent contractors earning up to ₹75 Lakhs annually, presumptive taxation permits declaring 50% of gross receipts as taxable profits, significantly simplifying compliance.
3. **GST & FIRC / FIRS:** Export of services from India requires obtaining a Goods and Services Tax (GST) registration with a Letter of Undertaking (LUT) to benefit from 0% export GST, along with Foreign Inward Remittance Certificates from your bank.

## AI Engineering: The New High-Growth Frontier

The fastest-accelerating compensation growth in India is concentrated in AI Systems and Machine Learning Infrastructure. Engineers who understand:
- Vector embeddings and RAG pipelines at scale
- Fine-tuning open weights models (Llama, DeepSeek, Mistral)
- Low-latency inference serving with vLLM and TensorRT-LLM

are commanding substantial salary premiums across domestic venture-backed startups and international remote contracts alike.
